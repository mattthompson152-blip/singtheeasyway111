Live, private one-to-one coaching on Microsoft Teams for contemporary and popular singers aged 16 and over.

Why this is different

A symptom is not the same as its cause

A tight high note, a lost phrase or an unreliable range can look similar from the outside. The cause may lie in different habits, pressure or coordination for each singer.

I listen to what happens in your voice and in the music before choosing an adjustment. The work is individual: understand the cause, test a useful change, then apply it in the song that matters to you.

Diagnosis first

Before choosing an exercise or technique change, Matt looks for the cause behind the recurring difficulty.

Matt's individual judgement

Similar symptoms can have different causes. The next step is based on what is happening in your voice, not a preset method.

How it works

What happens in a session

I listen to the voice as it is and identify the patterns that may be contributing to the difficulty.

I explain the cause and test a focused adjustment in context, then bring the work back into a real song.

The aim is useful, individual change without replacing what makes your voice sound like you.
