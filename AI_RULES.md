AI RULES

1. PURPOSE

These rules govern every AI agent, automated tool and contributor working on the Sing The Easy Way website repository.

Sing The Easy Way is an elite, diagnosis-led, owner-delivered vocal-coaching practice.

The website must express specialist judgement, individual attention, authority, discretion and high trust.

The website must not be described or positioned as merely premium.

“Premium” is not an approved description of:

Sing The Easy Way;
the website;
Matt Thompson;
the coaching;
the brand;
the commercial position;
the intended client experience.
The governing standard is elite.

This does not authorise unsupported prestige language, invented exclusivity, exaggerated credentials, guaranteed outcomes or empty luxury claims. Elite positioning must be demonstrated through specialist judgement, selective service structure, disciplined evidence, direct owner delivery, restrained presentation and clear commercial boundaries.

2. AUTHORITY ORDER

Resolve conflicts in this order:

Historical audits, old handovers, conversation exports, screenshots, cached pages, public search results and documents outside the repository are not current authority.

Do not infer the current website state from historical material.

If two active sources conflict, report the conflict. Do not guess or silently reconcile it.
