## 7. Approved services

### Consultation

£25, 30 minutes, live through Microsoft Teams, initial vocal assessment and recommendation, not credited against another service. Controlled diagnostic access, not a cheap trial, taster, starter lesson, complimentary assessment, free diagnosis or discount entry point.

### Private Vocal Coaching

£150 for one private 60-minute online session involving individual work on a particular problem, song or goal. No discounted block-booking rate.

### Development Programme

£1,400 paid upfront for one ten-session commitment. Ten completely individual sessions. Sessions 1–9 are 60 minutes; session 10 is 75 minutes and includes a 15-minute progress review and next-step plan. Valid for six months from the originally booked first-lesson date. Personal and non-transferable. Weekly sessions strongly recommended, fortnightly scheduling may work where necessary, and approximately two hours of independent practice each week is expected. No generic curriculum or guaranteed result.

### Professional Artist Coaching

Online, from £250 per hour, one-hour minimum, separate confidential enquiry, scope agreed before delivery and outside the ordinary consumer payment journey. May include agreed recording, performance, production, pronunciation, diction or industry support. Do not call it VIP or elite lessons.

Friendships, legacy arrangements and exceptional private agreements do not alter the published or standard professional-service position.

### Eligibility and delivery

Online through Microsoft Teams, contemporary and popular singing, age 16+, with parent or legal guardian purchase for a 16- or 17-year-old. Do not imply classical, operatic or ordinary in-person coaching.
