English pronunciation for artists singing in a second language

English pronunciation and diction work can be discussed as part of a separately scoped professional project.

Work may include pronunciation, diction, phrasing, delivery and performance, where agreed as part of the project scope.

The work is individual to the artist and the brief. Its aim is to support the performance without removing what makes the artist's voice recognisably their own.

Typically for

  • Recording an English language single, album track or film song
  • A song being released in more than one language
  • Crossover into English speaking markets
  • Screen roles where the performance has to be understood as well as heard
