Diagnosis before exercises

The strongest singers are not the ones who practice more. They are the ones who finally identify the actual blocker and remove it.

Most singers are taught to add more effort, more breath, more volume, more practice. Sometimes that is exactly what keeps the problem alive.

I listen for the pressure, the anticipation, the strain and the vocal habit that is getting in the way. Then we change the smallest thing that matters and test it inside a real song.

What you can work on

  • Range and consistency
  • Breath and vocal pressure
  • Confidence and performance
  • Vocal placement and resonance
  • Song interpretation
  • Recording and performance prep

How the lesson works

First 5 minutes

We check in on the voice, the way you are feeling and the specific problem you are trying to solve.

Next 10

I listen for tension, break points, pressure and habits that are keeping the voice stuck.

Next 10

We isolate the exact issue and explain what is happening in simple terms.

Next 20

We put the change back into a real song so it works in context, not just in isolation.

Last 5

We summarise what changed, what to practise and what to listen for next time.

Who this is for

Complete beginners

You are starting from zero and want clear, honest teaching with no confusion.

Developing singers

You can sing, but something keeps getting in the way: range, consistency, confidence or performance.

Artists and performers

You need a voice that can cope with pressure, repertoire, and a real performance brief.

What you receive after each session

  • Your lesson recording
  • A written summary of what we worked on
  • A clear practical plan for what to work on before the next lesson

This is part of the method. Nothing depends on memory or notes left in the wrong place.
