About Matt

Matt Thompson: vocal diagnostician and one-to-one coach

I did not set out to be a generic online singing teacher. I built a coaching practice around one question: what is actually stopping this voice from progressing?

Book Online

Diagnosis before technique

A lot of singers are told to practise harder, sing louder, or add more breath. The problem is often not a lack of effort. It is a misunderstanding about what is actually in the way.

I have worked with beginners, developing singers, recording artists, actors and performers. The common thread is simple: the voice is usually being held back by the same kind of obstacle in different forms.

That is why my method starts with listening carefully, identifying the real issue, and then making a small change that actually changes the sound.

How I Ended Up Teaching This Way

Over more than 25 years of teaching and coaching, I kept noticing the same thing: two singers can describe the same problem for completely different reasons. So the method I use today grew from listening for the specific obstacle holding back one individual singer, rather than giving everybody the same exercises.

Why generic exercises are not always the answer

Some singers arrive with plenty of exercises and plenty of information, and they are still stuck. Usually the underlying issue has not been identified. Another warm-up cannot fix a problem nobody has found yet, so finding it comes first.

Why individual judgement matters

My method relies on judgement, listening, clear explanation and application to your real music. I decide what to work on by what I hear in your voice, explain it plainly, and then we take it back into the songs you want to sing.

The same approach at every stage

I have worked with beginners, developing singers, performers, recording artists, actors and project-based professional clients, and I trained at the Academy of Contemporary Music. For a committed beginner, the work is finding what makes singing feel easier. For an experienced performer, it is precise, practical refinement. The approach does not change: listen first, then work on what matters.

Protecting your sound

I protect individuality rather than replacing it with a generic sound. The aim is for you to sound more like yourself, with more ease and control.

Recording, performance, diction and pronunciation support, where it is relevant to professional work, supports this coaching rather than overwhelming it, and is offered as a separate service.

What I actually do

Find the blocker

Whether it is strain, tension, breath pressure, range limitation, confidence, or vocal inconsistency, the work begins with diagnosis.

Fix the source

I aim for the smallest, clearest change that restores ease, range and freedom without flattening the singer's individuality.

Return to the song

We do not stay trapped in drills. We take the fix back into the actual music you want to sing and make it work in context.

The professional side

I also work with performers and artists who need support for recordings, project work, diction, performance and delivery under pressure. That work sits alongside coaching, but it is handled as a separate professional service.
