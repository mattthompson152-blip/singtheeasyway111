Personal coaching built around your voice

Every singer is different. Your coaching will be adapted to your experience, your goals and the specific challenges you are facing.

The work can include vocal technique, confidence, pitch, range, breath control, performance, preparation for recordings or productions, and finding a sound that feels natural and individual to you.

This is live, personal coaching delivered in real time. It is not a pre-recorded course or a generic programme.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need previous singing experience?

No. Lessons are suitable for complete beginners as well as developing singers, experienced performers, recording artists and actors.

Are the lessons online?

Yes. Lessons are live and one-to-one using Microsoft Teams. You work directly with Matt in real time. These are not pre-recorded lessons.

Which option should I choose?

Choose the consultation if you would like to discuss your voice, goals or lesson options first. Choose a single lesson if you are ready to begin, or the 10-lesson block if you want ongoing coaching and consistent development.

What happens after I book?

After you complete your booking through the secure booking form, Matt sends your lesson details by email, including your Microsoft Teams link and the information you need for your session.

Can I ask a question before booking?

Yes. Use the contact page if you would like to ask anything before choosing or paying for a session.
