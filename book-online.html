Diagnosis before exercises

Start with a clear view of your voice

When the cause of a vocal problem is unclear, begin with an individual assessment rather than choosing an exercise or programme by guesswork.

Consultation

The Consultation is controlled, gated diagnostic access to Matt Thompson's expertise. It identifies the principal vocal issue, assesses technical compatibility and establishes the most appropriate next action.

£25 for 30 minutes, live on Microsoft Teams. It includes an initial vocal assessment and recommendation and is not credited against another service.

Other coaching arrangements

Private Vocal Coaching: £150 for one private 60-minute online session, with individual work on a specific problem, song or goal. There is no discounted block-booking rate. Request Private Vocal Coaching.

Development Programme: £1,400 paid upfront for one ten-session commitment. Every session is individual; there is no generic curriculum or guaranteed result.

  • Sessions 1–9 are 60 minutes; session 10 is 75 minutes and includes a 15-minute progress review and next-step plan.
  • Valid for six months from the originally booked first-lesson date; personal and non-transferable.
  • Weekly sessions are strongly recommended; fortnightly may work where necessary. Approximately two hours of independent practice each week is expected.
  • For 16- or 17-year-olds, a parent or legal guardian purchases and accepts the terms.
