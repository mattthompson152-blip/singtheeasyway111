Choosing a Warm-up for the Song Ahead

Updated 2 November 2026

By Matt Thompson

A warm-up can help you arrive at a musical task, but a fixed list does not suit every voice, day or song. Ask what you need to prepare.

Warm up for the task ahead

Notice the first demanding moment in the repertoire and choose a gentle activity related to it. A warm-up need not use maximum range, volume or effort to prove it is working.

Comfort matters more than a checklist

A routine is not a guarantee of vocal health or performance quality. For pain, persistent hoarseness or unusual voice change, stop and seek healthcare advice.

Connect preparation to the song

Start easily, keep the sound comfortable and move towards the song gradually. If an exercise does not connect to the music or makes the voice feel worse, leave it out.
