What to Expect From Your First Online Singing Lesson

Updated 2 November 2026

By Matt Thompson

A first session is a conversation and a chance to hear what you want to work on. It is not an audition, and one short excerpt cannot define your voice.

Start with your own goal

Bring a song, a question or a difficulty you have noticed. The coach can listen, ask what you experience and suggest a focused adjustment to test in the music.

A lesson is not an audition

Coaching is delivered live through Microsoft Teams. It is not asynchronous recording feedback, and technology does not remove every possible difficulty.

Know what the paid Consultation includes

Choose a song you know and identify one moment to explore. A stable device, camera and microphone are useful; expensive recording equipment is not a stated requirement.

Bring enough context to make the question clear

Before joining, note the song, the section you want to discuss and what you notice when you sing it. You do not need a polished performance or a long list of goals. If you are not sure how to describe the issue, play or sing the relevant phrase and explain what you hoped would happen.

The coach can ask questions and offer a musical adjustment to consider, but a short consultation cannot establish a full history or guarantee a result. It is reasonable to ask what the session can and cannot address, and to decide afterward whether you want further coaching. A first conversation is not a commitment to buy a programme.

Prepare a focused first session
