Being Told You Could Not Sing As A Child

Ask a room of adults who cannot sing and most of them will trace it to primary school. A teacher, a choir audition, being asked to mime. It is remarkable how durable those moments are, and how rarely they were accurate.

Children's voices are unfinished, not bad

A child being assessed is a voice halfway through construction. Range is limited, coordination is developing, the ear is still calibrating. Judging that as a final product is like judging a building from the scaffolding.

Many children labelled as non-singers were simply behind on a curve they later caught up with. Nobody ever went back and told them.

So they carried a nine year old's assessment into their forties.

I have met very few people who genuinely cannot learn to sing. In 25 years, a handful. I have met a great many who were convinced of it by somebody unqualified to say so.

Why it lasted so long

Because you never tested it again. The belief was formed and then carefully protected by avoidance. You did not sing at school, so you did not sing at university, so you did not sing at karaoke, so you never accumulated any evidence to the contrary.

A belief that never gets challenged never gets updated. It just sits there getting older and more certain.

Meanwhile the instrument finished growing, and nobody checked.

Adults learn differently, and often better

There is a persistent idea that you have to start young. For professional level technique from scratch there is something to it. For learning to sing well, there is not.

Adults bring attention, patience, and the ability to understand an explanation rather than just copy a sound. They can tell me what something felt like, which children usually cannot. That makes teaching them considerably easier.

Most of the beginners I teach are adults. It is not a consolation prize, it is the main event.

Do not audition for me. That is what the school experience trained you to do and it is the worst possible way to start. We are just introducing your voice to me. Nothing is being decided.

What to do with the memory

Name it. Say it out loud to somebody who is qualified to have an opinion. Half its power comes from never having been examined.

Then get actual evidence. Not reassurance from a friend, a real change in your own voice that you can hear happening. That is what updates a thirty year old belief, and nothing else does.

Where to start as an adult

Do not begin by testing whether you can sing. That is the school experience again and it will produce the same result, because you will brace for a verdict.

Begin with something you already half know and quite like. One verse. Sung badly on purpose, if that helps, because deliberately singing badly removes the fear of accidentally singing badly.

Then get one piece of real information about your voice from somebody qualified. Not a verdict, information. What it is doing, what is in the way, what would change it. That is a completely different experience from being assessed, and for most people it is the first time they have had it.

What I would do in your lesson

I would want to hear the story, briefly, because it usually explains the habits I am about to hear. Then we would work on something small and specific, and you would hear it change. That does more for a thirty year old belief than any amount of encouragement.
