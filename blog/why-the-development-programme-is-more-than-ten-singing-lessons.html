Why the Development Programme Is More Than Ten Singing Lessons

First published 19 November 2026

By Matt Thompson

A single singing lesson can create an important change. The harder question is whether that change survives the next song, rehearsal or performance.

The Sing The Easy Way Development Programme is for singers who want continuity rather than ten disconnected appointments. Across ten individual online sessions, I follow how the voice responds, recognise recurring problems and test whether changes remain available when the music becomes more demanding.

I do not use a fixed library of exercises and force every singer through the same method. I listen to the voice, repertoire, and decisions the singer is trying to make. An exercise is chosen because it addresses evidence. It is tested inside the song, refined when necessary and revisited until the singer understands what changed and why.

That continuity matters. A useful adjustment can disappear under pressure, fail on a different vowel or become unreliable at performance intensity. Connected sessions allow us to diagnose those changes instead of starting again.

The programme is delivered personally by me through Microsoft Teams. Sessions one to nine last 60 minutes. The final 75-minute session includes a 15-minute progress review, giving the singer an account of what has developed and what should come next.

This is not a discounted bundle or a generic online course. It is a structured period of private vocal development for contemporary and popular singers who want their technique and interpretation followed over time.

After 25 years of coaching singers, I do not believe progress comes from collecting exercises. It comes from identifying the right problem, testing the right change and staying with the work long enough for the singer to own it.
