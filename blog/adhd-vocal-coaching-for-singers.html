ADHD Vocal Coaching for Singers: Turning Attention into Artistic Choices

First published 17 November 2026

By Matt Thompson

Singers with ADHD do not always need more discipline. They often need vocal coaching that gives attention a reason to engage.

In online vocal coaching, I use one precise problem at a time: an audible target, a purposeful task and a direct connection to the song. That might mean identifying the vowel disrupting a phrase, choosing where the lyric needs more weight or reducing several instructions to the single change that matters most.

ADHD can bring valuable qualities to contemporary singing. Some singers notice unusual patterns, make original connections and become absorbed when the work feels relevant. But repetition, organisation, transitions and completing practice can still create difficulty. Good coaching must support both sides.

Sing The Easy Way provides private, diagnosis-led online vocal coaching for contemporary and popular singers, including singers with ADHD and other neurodivergent profiles. Sessions are delivered individually through Microsoft Teams, with tasks and communication adapted to the singer rather than imposed through a generic exercise programme.
