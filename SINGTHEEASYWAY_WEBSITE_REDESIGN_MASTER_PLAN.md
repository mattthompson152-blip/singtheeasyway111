## 7. Lesson recording strategy

Principles
- Recordings are a learning tool, not a marketing stunt.
- Record only with explicit consent.

Implementation
- Offer recordings when booked via Teams. Confirm permissions in booking modal. Store recordings securely and link them only to the student account or private link.
- Retention policy: document in privacy notice and explain how to request deletion.

---

## 8. Lesson transcript strategy

Principles
- Transcripts are searchable memory aids.
- Transcripts must be accurate enough to locate corrections and exercises; they are not presented as verbatim legal records.

Implementation
- Offer transcripts when recording and transcription permissions are granted. Provide a searchable transcript file alongside the recording.
- Provide guidance on how to use the transcript (search for keywords like breathing, vowel, placement) in the lesson summary and emails.

---

## 9. Lesson summary strategy

Principles
- Each lesson ends with 3 to 5 clear action points.
- Summaries are short, measurable and prioritised.

Implementation
- Deliver a one page lesson summary after every lesson with: 1) What was corrected, 2) Three action points, 3) Suggested exercises and tempo, 4) Suggested schedule for the week.
- Include quick links in the summary to the exact time in the recording (deep links) and to transcript search.
