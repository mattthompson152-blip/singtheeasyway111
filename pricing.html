Common questions about price

Why is the consultation half price?

Because asking someone to spend £50 before they have heard me teach is a lot. £25 lets you find out whether I am the right coach for you, and there is no pressure to book anything afterwards.

Do I have to buy a block?

No. Plenty of people book single lessons for months. The block saves £150 and adds a long-term plan and email support, but it is there if you want it, not a requirement.

How often should I have lessons?

At the start, weekly or fortnightly. Monthly tends to stall progress early on, because the gap is long enough to lose momentum. Once things are established, people often spread them out.

Is there anything else to pay?

No. There is no joining fee, no subscription, no materials to buy. The lesson folder and everything in it is included.

What if I need to cancel?

Consultations need 24 hours notice and lessons need 48 hours. Full details are in the terms.
