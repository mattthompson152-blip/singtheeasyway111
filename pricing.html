Diagnosis before exercises

Understand the work before you choose a service

Each service is individual and delivered live online by Matt Thompson. The starting point is understanding what is happening in your voice and what kind of work is appropriate.

Request a Consultation

What you are paying for

The work begins with an individual assessment, not a preset exercise sequence. Matt identifies the cause behind the difficulty, explains the judgement and tests a useful adjustment in the music you want to sing.

Choose the service that fits the work required: an initial assessment, one focused session, a longer individual commitment or confidential professional project support.

Coaching services

Consultation

The Consultation is controlled, gated diagnostic access to Matt Thompson's expertise. It identifies the principal vocal issue, assesses technical compatibility and establishes the most appropriate next action.

£25 for 30 minutes, live on Microsoft Teams. It includes an initial vocal assessment and recommendation and is not credited against another service.

Private Vocal Coaching

£150 for one private 60-minute online session. Work is individual to a specific problem, song or goal. There is no discounted block-booking rate.

Development Programme

£1,400 paid upfront for one ten-session commitment. Every session is individual; there is no generic curriculum and no guaranteed result.

Practical questions

Why start with a consultation?

The Consultation identifies the principal vocal issue, assesses technical compatibility and provides an initial recommendation before you decide on any further work.

Do I need to buy a programme?

No. Private Vocal Coaching sessions are available and often work well for focused, specific issues. The programme is for singers who want structure and consistent progress over time.

Do I need to be a beginner?

No. The work suits beginners, developing singers, performers and professionals who need a clear, direct route forward.

Which platform do you use?

All lessons are delivered live on Microsoft Teams.

Is there a block-booking rate?

Private Vocal Coaching is £150 per 60-minute session with no discounted block-booking rate. The Development Programme is a separate ten-session commitment at £1,400 paid upfront.
