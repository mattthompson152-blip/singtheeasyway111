Common questions about price

Why start with a consultation?

A consultation gives you a proper diagnosis before you commit. It is a clear way to understand what is actually holding the voice back.

Do I need to buy a programme?

No. Single lessons are available and often work well for focused, specific issues. The programme is for singers who want structure and consistent progress over time.

Do I need to be a beginner?

No. The work suits beginners, developing singers, performers and professionals who need a clear, direct route forward.
