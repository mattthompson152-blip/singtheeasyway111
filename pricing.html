What the Development Programme includes

It is designed for singers who want structure, consistency and accountability rather than random lesson-by-lesson guesswork.

  • Ten live one-to-one coaching sessions
  • Ten completely individual sessions; never a generic curriculum
  • Sessions 1–9 last 60 minutes; session 10 lasts 75 minutes
  • Session 10 includes a 15-minute progress review and next-step plan
  • Weekly sessions are strongly recommended; fortnightly may work where necessary
  • Approximately two hours of independent practice each week is expected
  • Available from age 16; for a 16- or 17-year-old, a parent or legal guardian purchases and accepts the terms
  • Personal and non-transferable; no guaranteed result

Common questions about price

Why start with a consultation?

A consultation gives you a proper diagnosis before you commit. It is a clear way to understand what is actually holding the voice back.

Do I need to buy a programme?

No. Private Vocal Coaching sessions are available and often work well for focused, specific issues. The programme is for singers who want structure and consistent progress over time.

Do I need to be a beginner?

No. The work suits beginners, developing singers, performers and professionals who need a clear, direct route forward.

Which platform do you use?

All lessons are delivered live on Microsoft Teams.

Is there a block-booking rate?

Private Vocal Coaching is £150 per 60-minute session with no discounted block-booking rate. The Development Programme is a separate ten-session commitment at £1,400 paid upfront.
