Development Programme

A ten-session commitment shaped around your voice

Recurring vocal problems can have different causes, even when they sound alike. Matt assesses what is happening in your voice before deciding what work is appropriate.

The Development Programme provides time to test individual adjustments in real music, review what is changing and decide what needs attention next. The work is shaped around your voice, goals and songs; it is not a generic curriculum and has no guaranteed result.

One ten-session commitment: £1,400 paid in full upfront. Sessions 1–9 are 60 minutes; session 10 is 75 minutes, including a 15-minute progress review and next-step plan. Live on Microsoft Teams.

Apply for the Development Programme

Who the Development Programme is for

  • You know your voice is capable of more, but progress has stalled.
  • You improve during a lesson and then lose the improvement afterwards.
  • The same range, tension, confidence or consistency problem keeps returning.
  • You want a clear plan rather than disconnected lessons.
  • You want accountability and continuity.
  • You are prepared to work between sessions.

The programme is available from age 16. For a 16- or 17-year-old, a parent or legal guardian must purchase the programme and accept its terms. The programme may not be necessary if you have one isolated issue to work on; Private Vocal Coaching may be the better option.

A familiar symptom does not reveal the cause

Range, breath, pitch and performance difficulties can have different causes. Repeating a generic exercise may not address the particular pattern behind a recurring problem.

The work starts with what is happening in your voice, then tests adjustments in the music and situations that matter to you. There is no guaranteed result; the work is specific to the individual singer.

Work shaped by your individual assessment

There is no preset sequence of exercises or fixed curriculum. Matt's judgement of your voice, the issue being addressed and the music you bring determines what each session focuses on.

Adjustments are tested in context and reviewed as the work develops. Session 10 lasts 75 minutes and includes a 15-minute progress review and next-step plan.

Lesson notes and materials

Where the automated note service is used, Microsoft Teams transcription supports personalised lesson notes and a practice plan. Transcription is required for that service; lesson recording is separate and optional. Materials may be held in Microsoft 365 and private lesson folders. See the Privacy Policy for details.

What the work may address

  • Understanding patterns behind a recurring vocal difficulty.
  • Testing individual adjustments in the songs and situations that matter to you.
  • Identifying practice that is relevant to the work being done.
  • Reviewing progress and agreeing a next step in session 10.

Programme commitment and scheduling

The Development Programme is one ten-session commitment, not ten separately purchased or separately refundable lessons. The fee is paid in full upfront.

The programme is valid for six months from the originally booked first-lesson date. Weekly sessions are strongly recommended; fortnightly sessions may work where necessary. Approximately two hours of independent practice each week is expected.

Sessions are completely individual and live through Microsoft Teams. There is no generic curriculum and no guaranteed result. The programme is personal and non-transferable.

Programme questions

Why choose the programme instead of Private Vocal Coaching?

Choose Private Vocal Coaching for one focused issue. Choose the programme when a problem keeps returning, progress has stalled, or you want a structured body of work with consistent follow-through.

Can I start with a consultation?

Yes. The Consultation is controlled, gated diagnostic access to Matt Thompson's expertise. It lasts 30 minutes, is live on Microsoft Teams, includes an initial vocal assessment and recommendation, costs £25 and is not credited against another service.

Is the programme suitable for beginners?

Yes, if you want structured development. The work starts from your voice as it is and is adapted to you.

How much progress should I expect?

Progress varies by singer, the issue being addressed, attendance, practice and how established existing habits are. There is no fixed result or timetable.

How long do I have to use the sessions?

The programme is valid for six months from the originally booked first-lesson date. See the Terms.
