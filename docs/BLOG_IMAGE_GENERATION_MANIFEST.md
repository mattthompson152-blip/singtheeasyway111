## Shared production specification

Generate one distinct master image for each article below. Use a premium contemporary vocal-coaching environment, believable professional equipment where shown, restrained cinematic contrast, charcoal and warm-neutral colour with restrained warm orange, natural skin and anatomy, and credible candid composition. The visual should support the particular article rather than imitate a generic stock coaching session.

All prompts use a landscape **3:2** master at **2400 × 1600 px**. Keep the focal subject near the centre (approximately 50% x / 48% y). Keep all essential content inside the central 80% of width and central 75% of height, with additional breathing room at the edges. This protects the composition for the site's 16:9 desktop, 4:3 tablet and 3:2 mobile hero crops and the wider fixed-height card crop. Use the same approved master as article hero, blog card and Open Graph image unless a separately justified need arises.

**Negative constraints for every prompt:** no generated text, letters, labels, legible interface, fake logo, watermark, private student information, classical/operatic or theatrical costume, medical imagery presented as fact, invented vocal anatomy, distorted hands or faces, implausible studio equipment, excessive luxury cues, generic posed teacher-and-student stock photography, or essential details at crop edges. Reject visible errors rather than assuming they will be hidden by a crop.

Filename convention: the proposed output ends in `-0211.jpg`, using 2 November 2026 as the planned publication date for these replacement images. This is not a claim about an article's original publication date. If an image is published on another date, use the actual image publication day and month in the `DDMM` slot required by `prepare-image.py`. The output is a proposed name only; check that it does not already exist before processing.

All image cards should use the current article's descriptive alt text below, with intrinsic width and height taken from the processed file. Hero and Open Graph treatments use the same image and maintain the central crop-safe focal point. None of the current articles genuinely requires a diagram; add one only if a later editorial review identifies a distinction that cannot be explained clearly in prose.
